Valve Bias Pro Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Valve Bias Pro handles information when you use the Android app. Valve Bias Pro is a workshop utility for valve amplifier technicians, including bias calculations, valve and amplifier reference information, saved workshop jobs and related tools.

1. Information we collect

Valve Bias Pro does not require you to create an account and does not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

The app may store information that you enter or create on your device, including:

  • saved amplifier jobs and measurement readings;
  • custom amplifier records;
  • workshop notes;
  • favourites and app preferences; and
  • photos that you choose to add to amplifier records.

This information is used to provide the app's workshop features and is stored locally on your device unless you choose to export or share it.

2. Purchases and Google Play Billing

Valve Bias Pro may offer a one-time Pro purchase through Google Play Billing. Google processes payment information and purchase transactions. Valve Bias Pro does not receive or store your full payment card or bank details.

The app may check Google Play purchase entitlement so that Pro features can be activated or restored. Google's handling of payment and account information is governed by Google's own privacy policies and terms.

3. How your information is used

Information stored by Valve Bias Pro is used only to provide app functionality, such as saving jobs, displaying your notes, remembering favourites, attaching your chosen photos and restoring purchased Pro access.

4. Sharing and disclosure

Valve Bias Pro does not sell your personal information. The app does not provide your locally stored workshop information to advertisers or analytics providers.

If you deliberately use an export or share feature, the information you select is passed to the Android sharing destination that you choose. Once shared to another app or service, that destination's privacy practices apply.

5. Photos and files

If you choose to add an amplifier photo or export a workshop file, Valve Bias Pro only uses the relevant file or photo for the feature you requested. User-added photos are not uploaded to a Valve Bias Pro server.

6. Data retention and deletion

Locally saved app information remains on your device until you delete it using available app controls, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Device or operating-system backup settings may affect whether some app data is backed up or restored.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to keep the app's handling of information limited to what is needed for its features. No method of electronic storage is completely risk-free, so users should avoid storing unnecessary sensitive personal information in workshop notes.

8. Children's privacy

Valve Bias Pro is a specialist technical/workshop utility and is not specifically directed to children. The app does not knowingly operate a service intended to collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
